PROTOCOL TITLE: A Phase II Study of Compound TNX-202 in HER2 Positive Breast Cancer
PHASE: Phase 2
TARGET ENROLLMENT: 120
INDICATION: Breast Cancer

SCHEDULE OF ASSESSMENTS (excerpt):
Day 1: infusion, ECG, safety labs.
Day 21: tumor assessment.

INCLUSION CRITERIA:
1. Female patients aged >= 18 years.
2. Histologically confirmed HER2 positive breast cancer.
3. ECOG Performance Status of 0 or 1.
4. Hemoglobin >= 9.0 g/dL.

EXCLUSION CRITERIA:
1. Prior treatment with trastuzumab emtansine.
2. Pregnant or breastfeeding women.
