PROTOCOL TITLE: A Phase III Study of Compound TNX-101 in Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)
PHASE: Phase 3
TARGET ENROLLMENT: 450
INDICATION: Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

SCHEDULE OF ASSESSMENTS (excerpt):
Day 1: PK blood draws x6, ECG, safety labs.
Day 14 +/- 2 days: tumor assessment, PK trough.
Day 16: safety follow-up visit.

INCLUSION CRITERIA:
1. Male or female patients aged >= 18 years and <= 75 years.
2. Histologically or cytologically confirmed metastatic NSCLC with documented EGFR Exon 19 deletion or L858R mutation.
3. ECOG Performance Status of 0 or 1.
4. Adequate bone marrow reserve: Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) >= 1.5 x 10^9/L, Platelets >= 100 x 10^9/L, Hemoglobin >= 9.0 g/dL.
5. Serum creatinine <= 1.5x Upper Limit of Normal (ULN) or calculated CrCl >= 50 mL/min.

EXCLUSION CRITERIA:
1. Prior third-generation EGFR TKI therapy within 180 days of Day 1.
2. Active brain metastases or spinal cord compression. Asymptomatic treated brain metastases are allowed if stable for >= 28 days without corticosteroids.
3. Clinically significant cardiovascular disease: QTc interval > 470 msec, Class II-IV heart failure.
4. Pregnant or breastfeeding women.
