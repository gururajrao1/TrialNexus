PROTOCOL TITLE: A Phase II Study of Compound TNX-303 in Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
PHASE: Phase 2
TARGET ENROLLMENT: 200
INDICATION: Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

SCHEDULE OF ASSESSMENTS (excerpt):
Day 1: safety labs, ECG.
Day 28: HbA1c, safety labs.

INCLUSION CRITERIA:
1. Male or female patients aged >= 18 years.
2. Diagnosed type 2 diabetes mellitus.
3. HbA1c >= 7.0% at screening.

EXCLUSION CRITERIA:
1. Pregnant or breastfeeding women.
2. Severe renal impairment: eGFR < 30 mL/min.
